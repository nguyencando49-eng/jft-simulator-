## Lesson boundary

### Situation

Meeting someone for the first time and exchanging basic personal information.

### Communication objectives / Can-do

- Introduce oneself with a name and nationality.
- Ask and answer another person's name politely.
- Say where one comes from.
- Confirm simple personal information with a yes/no question.
- Say that two people share, or do not share, the same basic attribute.
- Use a short first-meeting greeting appropriately.

### Grammar boundary

- `N は N です`
- `N は N じゃありません`
- `N は N ですか`
- `N も N です`
- `N の N`
- `～から来ました`
- Question words for basic identity/origin: `だれ`, `どちら`, `どこ`

### Vocabulary boundary

- Personal identity: `わたし`, `なまえ`, `人`, `ともだち`
- Origin/nationality: `国`, `出身`, `日本`, `ベトナム`, `韓国`, `～人`
- Workplace relationship: `会社`, `同じ`, `みなさん`
- Basic comprehension: `日本語`, `わかります`
- Polite encounter expressions: `はじめまして`, `よろしくお願いします`, `失礼ですが`

### Kanji boundary

- 私
- 人
- 日
- 本
- 国
- 名
- 前
- 会
- 社
- 同
- 出
- 身

### Expressions

- `はじめまして。`
- `どうぞよろしくお願いします。`
- `失礼ですが、お名前は？`
- `ご出身はどちらですか。`
- `～から来ました。`
- `はい、そうです。`
- `いいえ、～じゃありません。`

### Suitable JFT practice skills

- Script and Vocabulary
- Conversation and Expression
- Listening
- Reading

### Excluded required knowledge

- Detailed addresses, dates of birth, occupations, family relations, honorific business titles, and cultural facts not explicitly included above.
- Grammar beyond simple nominal predicates, yes/no questions, `も`, possession/association with `の`, and origin with `から来ました`.
