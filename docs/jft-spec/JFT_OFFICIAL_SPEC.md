# JFT-Basic official specification

Status: official-domain facts, verified against Japan Foundation pages on 2026-08-17.

## Purpose and level

JFT-Basic measures Japanese ability needed by non-native speakers to communicate in everyday situations in Japan, particularly people coming mainly for work. It measures what a learner can do in Japanese rather than isolated academic knowledge.

Questions are based on Can-do statements at CEFR/JF Standard A1–A2. The August 2026 reporting change does not change the testing method, test structure, or question level.

## Method, structure, and navigation

- CBT; approximately 50 questions in 60 minutes; no independent section time limit.
- Script and Vocabulary (~12): word meaning/usage and kanji reading/meaning/usage.
- Conversation and Expression (~12): grammar and expression.
- Listening (~12): conversations, shops/public places, announcements/instructions.
- Reading (~12): comprehending content and information search.
- Review/change is allowed within a non-listening section, but a completed section cannot be revisited.
- Listening has no backward/forward review navigation; audio may be played up to two times.

## Result reporting from August 2026

| Official scaled score | Official assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Below 145 | No assessment displayed (`*`) |
| 145–174 | A1 |
| 175–199 | A2.1 |
| 200–250 | A2.2 (A2) |

A2.2 corresponds to the A2 assessment used through July 2026. A2.1 means the test taker has reached A1 and is progressing toward A2.2.

The official score range is 10–250. It is not a simple sum or percentage of correct answers; it is a scaled score calculated using statistical equating. Section percentages are reference information.
